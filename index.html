01 / THE PROTOCOL

From information to evidence.

Vortex does not store files, models, or documents. It records minimal cryptographic fingerprints so anyone can verify integrity, authorship, and history.

#

LOCAL HASH

The file stays with you. Only its mathematical fingerprint is prepared for registration.

◇

COMMITMENT

Millions of records can be condensed into a single 32-byte Merkle root.

V

PoW INCLUSION

The commitment enters a Vortex block with an inclusion proof and accumulated work.

✓

PORTABLE RECEIPT

Offline verification with no account, company, server, or mandatory node. Evidence that outlives its issuer.

11 / FAQ

Direct answers. No fog.

The fastest way to understand Vortex today is to separate what exists from what is still being built.

Does Vortex have a token?

No. There is currently no Vortex token sale, public token, wallet, mainnet, or financial product.

Is the Vortex testnet public?

No. Node testing is currently controlled and private. A public testnet will be announced only after operator procedures and readiness gates are met.

Does Vortex store my files or data?

The intended proof flow keeps files, models, and documents with their owner. Minimal cryptographic commitments are used to verify integrity and history.

What works today?

Vortex has a tested research core, an implemented architectural direction, a browser-based local hash demonstration, and active controlled node testing. It is not production software.

How can developers participate?

Developers and researchers can introduce themselves by email and follow the public GitHub profile. Broader contribution paths will open with stable documentation and public repositories.

Is supporting Vortex an investment?

No. Support does not provide tokens, equity, ownership, governance rights, or an expectation of financial return.
